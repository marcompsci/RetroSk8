Push, pop and flow through a retro arcade skate world built from scratch.

STRING THE LINE
Flip, grab and shove in the air, then grind rails and ledges, stall on coping, ride walls, bonk anything in your way and manual between it all. Keep the line going to stack your multiplier, then land clean to bank it.

TEN PARKS AND A WHOLE CITY
Session Harbor Plaza, Neon Warehouse, Rooftop Run, Sunset Bowls, Floodgate Ditch, Moonlight Pier, Twin Screen Drive-In, the Off-Season Rink, the Shipyard and the Subway Depot. Then explore Retro City: find spots, collect tapes, race the streets and take on the daily City Jam.

NIGHT SESSIONS
Skate any park at dusk or after dark, with neon, street lights and a night mix of every song. Each park has a Night Line to clear for new stickers.

A STORY TO FINISH
Join a local crew and stop a slick sponsored crew from locking up the city's best spots, then take on the drive-in's Projectionists, the Rink Rats and the shipyard's Deckhands, and finish it all with the Originals. Ten chapters, told in comic-book panels.

MAKE IT YOURS
Customize your skater down to the socks, then design your own board in the Deck Designer: patterns, stickers you can move, size and turn, pixel paint and custom grip tape. Build your own tricks in Create-a-Trick and your own parks in Create-a-Park, and share any of them with a code.

RATED, RANKED, SLAMMED
One SK8 RATING from 0 to 10,000 measures your whole game, with every park counted the same. Enter the weekly Spot Contests against five rivals, tune your trucks and wheels, watch your deck wear in, and when it all goes wrong, the Hall of Meat scores your slams.

THE TRICK BOOK
Every trick in the game, with how to do it and four challenges each. Fill your book, earn Tape Tokens.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Touch controls you can rearrange, full controller support, iPad support, and no internet needed for the core game. Assists slow the game down, steady your balance and widen landings, and four colour-vision palettes keep the HUD readable.

SKATE WITH FRIENDS
Edit your replays with seven camera angles and auto slow-mo. Send a ghost of your run, trade park and challenge codes, play S.K.A.T.E. or pass the phone for party games, including Trick Battle. Game Center leaderboards for every park, race and spot.

Come back every day for the Daily Line, the Daily Trick and your streak. Your progress backs up to iCloud.

Optional cosmetic packs change your look only. No ads. No loot boxes. No tracking.
