How the beta works

  1. Install Apple's free TestFlight app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Open the beta link and tap Accept, then Install.
  3. Play. When something breaks or feels off, take a screenshot: TestFlight lets you send it to us with a note.

Weekly challenge

Every week there's a Deck of the Week and a ghost run to beat. Copy the code, open Retro Sk8 → CODES, paste it, and skate it. Deck codes go into Create-a-Skater → Board Art → Deck Designer → Paste Code.
